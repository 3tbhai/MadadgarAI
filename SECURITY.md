## 2. Official Statutory Scam Shield (घोटाला सुरक्षा गार्ड)

MadadgaarAI incorporates strict official portal validation mechanisms to shield students from fraudulent third-party scholarship aggregators and phishing sites:

1. **Free Application Guarantee**: All government scholarship portals (**NSP, AICTE, UGC, MahaDBT, UP Dashmottar**) are **100% free of cost**. Never pay any fee or registration charge to any middleman or unofficial website.
2. **Canonical Domain Verification**:
   - Central Schemes: Must terminate strictly in `.gov.in` or `.nic.in` (e.g. `scholarships.gov.in`, `aicte.gov.in`).
   - State Portals: Must originate from official state portals (e.g. `mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in`, `scholarship.up.gov.in`).
   - CSR Programs: Must link directly to legitimate registered non-profit trust domains (e.g. `reliancefoundation.org`, `kotakeducation.org`).
3. **Zero-Knowledge Privacy**: MadadgaarAI does not collect, record, or transmit Aadhaar numbers, biometric data, or banking authentication credentials.
