\chapter*{Abstract}
\thispagestyle{plain}
Discovering verified scholarships, corporate internships, and scientific research grants while navigating complex academic documentation remains a persistent challenge for students, researchers, and educators in India. Announcements are fragmented across statutory ministries, state portals, philanthropic trusts, and company hiring boards; critical eligibility rules are embedded inside unstructured web pages and multi-page PDF circulars; and learners lack an isolated, citation-backed study assistant capable of reasoning over their own technical documents without hallucination.

\textbf{MadadgaarAI (v4.0)} is a completed, production-ready opportunity intelligence and multi-tenant academic AI platform that solves both discovery and comprehension end to end. Building substantially beyond the initial mid-term prototype, the final system eliminates all hardcoded seed datasets and delivers:
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Zero-Mock Real-Time Scraping \& Anti-404 Verification}: 17 asynchronous live scrapers covering Indian government portals (DST INSPIRE, ISTI India, AICTE, ISRO RESPOND, ANRF/SERB, UGC, ICMR, BIRAC), philanthropic CSR trusts (Tata Trusts), international funding feeds (Grants.gov), and leading technology company boards (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Internshala), fortified with Playwright headless fallback, live HTTP link verification, expiration filtering, and background periodic auto-refresh.
    \item \textbf{On-Demand Deep-Page Circular Analyzer \& Proposal Drafter}: Fetches live destination web pages, structured JSON APIs, and PDF circulars to summarize eligibility criteria, stipends, deadlines, required document checklists, and application steps, paired with an 11-section Ministry of Finance-aligned proposal and SOP outline generator.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Modal Matching Suite}: Combines deterministic student scholarship eligibility evaluation, hybrid BM25 + Dense Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) + semantic reranking for faculty research grants, and a PDF/DOCX/text Resume Matcher spanning 80+ technical and scientific skills.
    \item \textbf{Personal Application Tracker \& Reminder Engine}: Provides Board and chronological Deadline Timeline views, personal notes, RFC~5545 iCalendar (\texttt{.ics}) export, filtered CSV export, URL-synced shareable views, and browser/webhook/email deadline reminders.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Tenant Saral AI Platform (Google OAuth~2.0 + Gemini API RAG)}: Secures private per-user knowledge bases behind Google OAuth~2.0 / OpenID Connect and HTTP-only JWT cookies with refresh-token rotation, an extensible role-based Mode Registry (\texttt{Student}, \texttt{Researcher}, \texttt{Educator}), layout-aware recursive chunking with per-user SHA-256 deduplication, official \texttt{google-genai} embeddings (\texttt{gemini-embedding-001}) and SSE-streamed grounded responses (\texttt{gemini-2.5-flash}), KaTeX math rendering, and clickable page-level source citations.
    \item \textbf{Production Infrastructure \& Polished Consumer Interface}: Ships with a PostgreSQL~16 \texttt{pgvector} HNSW migration alongside local SQLite persistence, Docker containerization, sliding-window rate limiting, structured JSON logging, GitHub Actions CI, and a consumer-polished 4-tab Neo-Brutalist web interface free of exposed internal technical artifacts.
\end{enumerate}

The completed platform comprises 49 Python modules (13,255 lines of source code), 45 REST/SSE endpoints, and 46 automated unit, security, and live-pipeline integration tests passing with a 100\% success rate, achieving an empirical retrieval Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) of \textbf{1.0000} and Hit@1 of \textbf{100.00\%} across live verified opportunities.

\chapter{Introduction}

\section{Background \& Motivation}
Access to timely financial aid, research funding, industry internships, and structured academic mentorship directly shapes higher education outcomes in India. Each year, substantial funding is announced by central ministries, statutory councils, scientific agencies, philanthropic CSR trusts, and global technology organizations:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Central \& Statutory Portals:} National Scholarship Portal (NSP), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), University Grants Commission (UGC), Department of Science \& Technology (DST INSPIRE), and India Science, Technology \& Innovation (ISTI) portal.
    \item \textbf{National Research \& Mission Agencies:} Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF / SERB), Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO RESPOND), Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC), and international agencies such as Grants.gov.
    \item \textbf{Corporate Boards \& Philanthropic Foundations:} Tata Trusts CSR, Internshala, and direct engineering/research internship boards hosted on Greenhouse, Ashby, and Lever (including Anthropic, Stripe, Airbnb, MongoDB, Datadog, Brex, Cloudflare, Discord, Scale AI, OpenAI, and Linear).
\end{itemize}

Students and researchers still encounter four systemic obstacles:
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Fragmented \& Volatile Listings:} Deadlines shift frequently, links expire into HTTP~404 errors, and static aggregator lists rapidly go stale.
    \item \textbf{Unstructured Requirements \& Compliance Friction:} Eligibility ceilings, required certificates, and procedural guidelines are buried inside dense HTML notices and PDF circulars, leading to avoidable disqualifications.
    \item \textbf{Disconnected Application Management:} Applicants juggle spreadsheets, browser bookmarks, and manual calendar alerts without unified tracking or resume-to-opportunity fit analysis.
    \item \textbf{Ungrounded Academic Study Tools:} Students and researchers preparing proposals, studying papers, or reviewing lecture PDFs need strictly grounded, citation-backed assistance tailored to their academic role rather than generic unverified chat output.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Problem Statement}
To design, engineer, harden, and validate a unified, production-ready web platform that continuously curates live verified internships, scholarships, and research grants with zero hardcoded mock data, provides explainable eligibility and resume matching with application tracking, and integrates a secure multi-tenant Gemini-powered Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) study workspace with role-based user modes and clickable page-level citations.

\section{Final Project Objectives Achieved}
All engineering and research objectives defined for the project have been fully realized in MadadgaarAI v4.0:
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=3pt]
    \item \textbf{Zero-Mock Live Opportunity Pipeline:} Replace all static seed datasets with 17 live asynchronous scrapers, Playwright headless rendering fallback, strict canonical URL validation, live HTTP link verification, expiration filtering, and background periodic synchronization.
    \item \textbf{On-Demand Deep Circular Intelligence \& Drafting:} Parse destination webpages, structured APIs, and PDF circulars in real time to extract stipends, deadlines, eligibility rules, document checklists, and application steps, integrated with an 11-section proposal/SOP outline generator.
    \item \textbf{Comprehensive Matching Suite:} Deliver Student Scholarship Eligibility matching, Faculty Research Grant matching via Hybrid BM25 + Dense RRF + Semantic Reranking, and PDF/DOCX/Text Resume-to-Opportunity matching.
    \item \textbf{Unified Application Lifecycle Management:} Build a persistent Board and Deadline Timeline tracker with notes, RFC~5545 \texttt{.ics} calendar exports, filtered CSV downloads, URL-synced shareable filters, and multi-channel deadline reminders.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Tenant Authentication \& Role-Based Modes:} Implement production Google OAuth~2.0 / OIDC sign-in with HTTP-only JWT cookies, refresh-token rotation and replay detection, strict per-user tenant isolation, and an extensible Strategy/Registry User Mode system (\texttt{Student}, \texttt{Researcher}, \texttt{Educator}).
    \item \textbf{Production Gemini RAG Workspace:} Integrate the official \texttt{google-genai} SDK (\texttt{gemini-embedding-001} and \texttt{gemini-2.5-flash}) with asynchronous layout-aware chunking, SHA-256 deduplication, hybrid RRF retrieval, SSE streaming, KaTeX math rendering, and clickable source passage inspection.
    \item \textbf{Production Readiness, Testing \& UX Polish:} Deliver PostgreSQL~16 \texttt{pgvector} migration parity, Docker containerization, per-user rate limiting, CI automation, 46 passing automated tests, and a clean consumer-facing web interface with all internal technical details abstracted away.

\section{Information Retrieval \& Grounded RAG Formulations}

\subsection{BM25 Okapi Lexical Scoring}
Exact technical terms, scheme acronyms (e.g., \texttt{ANRF}, \texttt{NPCI-DBT}, \texttt{GFR 2017}), and mathematical symbols are scored via BM25 Okapi \cite{robertson2009bm25}:
\begin{equation}
    \text{Score}_{\text{BM25}}(D, Q) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{IDF}(q_i) \cdot \frac{f(q_i, D) \cdot (k_1 + 1)}{f(q_i, D) + k_1 \cdot \left(1 - b + b \cdot \frac{|D|}{\text{avgdl}}\right)}
\end{equation}
where $f(q_i, D)$ is the frequency of query term $q_i$ in document or chunk $D$, $|D|$ is document length, $\text{avgdl}$ is corpus mean length, $k_1 = 1.5$, and $b = 0.75$.

\subsection{Task-Aware Dense Semantic Embeddings}
MadadgaarAI uses two complementary embedding pipelines:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Opportunity Corpus Embeddings:} Compact 384-dimensional normalized vectors (\texttt{all-MiniLM-L6-v2} \cite{reimers2019sentencebert} with deterministic local fallback) enabling sub-20~ms hybrid search over live opportunities.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Tenant Saral AI Embeddings:} Official Google Gemini \texttt{gemini-embedding-001} vectors \cite{gemini_api} generated with asymmetric task instructions (\texttt{task\_type="RETRIEVAL\_DOCUMENT"} during indexing and \texttt{task\_type="RETRIEVAL\_QUERY"} at query time).
\end{itemize}
Cosine similarity between query vector $\mathbf{e}_q$ and document or chunk vector $\mathbf{e}_d$ is computed as:
\begin{equation}
    \text{Sim}_{\text{Dense}}(q, d) = \frac{\mathbf{e}_q \cdot \mathbf{e}_d}{\|\mathbf{e}_q\|_2 \, \|\mathbf{e}_d\|_2}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) \& Semantic Reranking}
Lexical and semantic rankings are combined via Reciprocal Rank Fusion \cite{cormack2009rrf} with smoothing parameter $k = 60$:
\begin{equation}
    \text{RRF}(d) = \frac{1}{k + r_{\text{BM25}}(d)} + \frac{1}{k + r_{\text{Dense}}(d)}, \qquad \widehat{\text{RRF}}(d) = \min\!\left(1,\; \frac{\text{RRF}(d)}{2 / (k + 1)}\right)
\end{equation}
In the opportunity and resume matchers, candidate scores are further refined by a cross-feature semantic reranker (\texttt{TextEmbedder.rerank\_score}) combining token overlap, phrase precision, and base RRF alignment.

\subsection{Tenant-Isolated Grounded Generation}
For every multi-tenant Saral AI query by authenticated user $u$ in mode $m \in \{\text{student}, \text{researcher}, \text{educator}\}$, retrieval is strictly restricted to the tenant slice $\mathcal{C}_u = \{c \in \mathcal{C} \mid c.\text{user\_id} = u\}$. Retrieved top-$k_m$ chunks must pass an evidence gate (minimum dense similarity or lexical token overlap) before being passed to \texttt{gemini-2.5-flash} under a mode-specific system prompt requiring inline citations \texttt{[Source: Document, Page / Section]} and explicit abstention when uploaded files lack sufficient evidence.

\section{Security, Tenant Isolation \& Reliability Architecture}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Google OAuth~2.0 \& Server-Side OIDC Verification:} Supports both Authorization Code redirect flow with CSRF state cookies (\texttt{saral\_oauth\_state}) and Google Identity Services ID token verification via \texttt{google-auth} against \texttt{GOOGLE\_CLIENT\_ID} \cite{rfc6749}.
    \item \textbf{HTTP-Only JWT Cookies \& Refresh Rotation:} Access tokens (30-minute TTL) and refresh tokens (14-day TTL) \cite{rfc7519} are stored in \texttt{HttpOnly}, \texttt{SameSite=Lax} cookies. Rotating a refresh token or switching user modes atomically revokes the superseded \texttt{jti}; replaying a revoked token triggers immediate HTTP~401 rejection and revokes all active tokens for that user.
    \item \textbf{Mandatory Tenant Pre-Filtering:} Every document read, chunk inspection, deletion, and hybrid retrieval query enforces \texttt{WHERE user\_id = :current\_user\_id}, guaranteeing zero cross-user data leakage.
    \item \textbf{Rate Limiting, Input Validation \& Retry Backoff:} Sliding-window per-user rate limiters guard \texttt{/ingest} (25 req/min) and \texttt{/chat} (40 req/min); uploads enforce a 15~MB cap and strict extension/MIME whitelists (\texttt{.pdf}, \texttt{.md}, \texttt{.markdown}, \texttt{.txt}); Gemini embedding and streaming calls implement exponential backoff retries on HTTP~429/503 with deterministic grounded fallback.
    \item \textbf{Atomic Windows-Safe Storage:} Vector indices and processed caches persist via temporary file creation followed by \texttt{os.replace()}, eliminating file-locking race conditions on Windows and Linux hosts.
\end{itemize}

\section{Layout-Aware Chunking \& Streaming Gemini RAG}
The ingestion service (\texttt{src/rag/chunker.py} and \texttt{src/rag/service.py}) extracts per-page PDF text with heading detection, Markdown header sections, or cleaned web/arXiv DOM sections, then applies recursive paragraph-and-word-window chunking preserving \texttt{page\_number}, \texttt{section\_header}, \texttt{chunk\_index}, and estimated token counts. Ingestion executes asynchronously via FastAPI \texttt{BackgroundTasks}, updating persistent job states while deduplicating identical files per user via SHA-256.

During chat, \texttt{POST /api/saral/chat/stream} streams Server-Sent Events (SSE) in three structured frame types:
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
    \item \texttt{meta}: Returns the persisted \texttt{session\_id}, active mode, and ordered list of cited chunks (\texttt{doc\_id}, \texttt{chunk\_id}, \texttt{doc\_title}, \texttt{page\_number}, \texttt{section\_header}).
    \item \texttt{delta}: Streams incremental Markdown and LaTeX tokens generated by \texttt{client.models.generate\_content\_stream}.
    \item \texttt{done}: Signals completion, triggers client-side KaTeX math rendering, and persists the assistant message alongside \texttt{cited\_chunk\_ids}.
\end{enumerate}
